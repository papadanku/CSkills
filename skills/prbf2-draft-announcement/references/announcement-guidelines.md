
# Announcement & Update Highlights Guidelines

## Title Conventions

- **Major Release**: `Project Reality: BF2 v<X.Y> Released!`
- **Patch Highlights**: `v.<X.Y.Z> - Update Highlights` or `v<X.Y.Z> - Update Highlights`
- **Themed / Seasonal / Teaser**: `<Season/Event> Update` or `<Series Name> Part <I/II/III> - <Subtitle>`

## Tone & Voice

- **Celebratory & Grateful**: Major announcements open with pride in the team's milestone and gratitude toward the player base and donors.
- **Accessible & Narrative**: Unlike changelogs, announcements tell the story of *what* the update brings to the battlefield (theater, atmosphere, tactical flavor) rather than listing every file-level tweak.
- **Contributor Recognition**: Credit mappers, modelers, and contributors using their community tags (`[R-DEV]`, `[R-CON]`, `[R-COM]`) when highlighting specific assets or maps.

## Required Sections for Major Releases

1. **Header Banner & Community Intro**: Milestone numbers, years of development, and release context.
2. **Release Trailer**: Placeholder for the official YouTube trailer.
3. **Thematic Spotlights**: 2-4 focused sections (e.g., new faction, new vehicles, new maps) with screenshot grids.
4. **KEY FEATURES List**: Concise bullet list summarizing headline additions (`Added <item>.`), followed by a prominent link to the full Changelog thread.
5. **Standard Footer Blocks**:
    - Download & Installation
    - Official Manual & New Player Landing Page
    - Mod Support forum pointer
    - Server Files & Server License application link
    - Donation transparency statement (infrastructure/hosting only, zero team salaries)
    - Social media & forum call-to-action + `- The Project Reality Team`
